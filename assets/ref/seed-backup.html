Seed phrase backup cards

Four cards per sheet. Print, cut along the dashed lines, and hand one to each student before they create their seed phrase. Students write their 12 words on the card by hand and sign in from it every day.

Prints on Letter or A4, portrait, at 100% scale. Card stock works best. A class of 20 needs 5 sheets; print a spare sheet for mistakes.
