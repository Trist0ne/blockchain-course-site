Instructors

Everything for running this class. After setup, the class key file is the only thing you need.

During the class

Class results
Load the class key to see who has registered, grade, approve replaced seed phrases, and publish results to students.

Print seed phrase backup cards
Four to a sheet. Each student writes their 12 words on one.

A student lost their seed phrase

  1. If they are still signed in anywhere, their handle in the top bar opens "Your course identity", where they can show the words and copy them again.
  2. Otherwise they choose "Lost your seed phrase?" at sign-in, enter their handle and make a new seed phrase.
  3. Confirm in person that it is them, then press Accept on their handle in Class results. Their earlier work is kept, and the old words stop counting.

Setting up

Set up this class, or replace its inbox token
Once per class, by whoever has the course repo. Use it again if the token expires or leaks.
